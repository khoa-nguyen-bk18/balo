Engine Features

Built for Performance, Speed, & Absolute Privacy

Designed to overcome the limitations of default chat backups, giving you full control and ownership over your personal data.

CAS File Deduplication

Content-Addressable Storage (CAS) indexes files by their cryptographic hash. If a photo or video is shared 10 times across 10 group chats, it only takes up disk space once.

Silent Interval Backups

Configure automated background synchronization at regular intervals. Runs silently on Android WorkManager with Wi-Fi only or cellular triggers to preserve battery and quota.

Hardware PIN & Passphrase

Safeguard your backup archives with custom passphrases or Android hardware Keystore PIN protection. Your chats remain private even if external storage is accessed.

One-Click Reconstitution

Export or restore full conversation histories, voice notes, stickers, and attachments back into human-readable SQLite or file trees in seconds without data corruption.

Local-First Ownership

Your chat archives are stored on your local device, SD card, or your own private cloud storage. Zero telemetry on message contents, zero centralized server dependence.

Storage Health & Analytics

Inspect which chats occupy the most media space, clean orphaned temporary cache files, and optimize internal storage without deleting important conversation logs.
